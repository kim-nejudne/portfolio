Buzzer variations

Six red buzzers for the phone. Hover and press each one.

A. Dome

A glossy dome in a black base. The current mock.

B. Arcade

A flat dished cap on a tall wall, deep travel.

C. Mushroom

Seen from the side, the cap wider than its plinth.

D. Printed

The dome set in the teal and orange passes.

E. Lit

A bulb glows under the dome while buzzers are open.

F. Disc

Flat red and an ink outline, depth by offset alone.
